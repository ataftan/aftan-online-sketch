The Kitaab Kollective Manifesto is a collaborative publishing project done alongside Anukriti, who co-runs the collective with me. This link is the collective’s website which I am in the process of coding. Our manifesto lays down our vision and intentions with the collective, and how we aim to build transnational artistic solidarity through media literacy, dialogue and artistic exchange. In working on this project, we see knowledge-building as producing a collective critical consciousness, rooted in love, unity, comradery. Our collaborative writing and envisioning practice relies on a dialogue and research-based process. Supported by the Collective Future's Fund, the Kitaab Kollective Manfiesto has been exhibited at the Boston Art Book Fair and Cultural Equity Incubator.
