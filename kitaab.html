Kitaab Kollective Manifesto, 2023
Research-based Publication

Kitaab Kollective is an artist collective that seeks to act as a metaphorical vessel, to build antidotes to historical amnesia, focusing on media and historical literacy, through publications as one of our mediums. The Kitaab Kollective Manifesto is a collaborative publishing project, where we lay down our vision and intentions with the collective, and how we aim to build transnational artistic solidarity through media literacy, dialogue and artistic exchange. Our collaborative writing and envisioning practice relies on a dialogue and research-based process of speculation. Supported by the Collective Future's Fund, the publication has been exhibited at the Boston Art Book Fair and Cultural Equity Incubator.
