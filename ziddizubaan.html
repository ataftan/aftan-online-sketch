Ziddi Zubaan, 2024
Publication concept

Ziddi Zubaan is a publication project that attempts to employ diy web-based archiving and publishing practices through collaboration and play. Through this open call we aim to curate and compose an archive of artistic submissions that engage with media literacy as a tool against censorship. In the present time, we are witnessing a repression of dissent across autocratic regimes. In India (and beyond), censorship induces an altering of history, endangering our cultural heritage. Through forms of collective knowledge formation we can set out to resist historical erasure.
