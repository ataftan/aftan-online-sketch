Digital Ruins/ Palimpsest, 2024
Web Sculpture

digital ruins / palimpsest explores divinatory possibilities of browser-based opacity through Python-generated text and disobedient typeface. The site, acting as ‘sculptural palimpsest’, reshapes language through ritualistic processes of insertion and disfiguration, forming an incubatory vessel for rebellious & ephemeral intimacies.
