Phosphene Imprints, 2024
Web Sculpture

Phosphene Imprints is an instruction on receiving time. Drawing from the psychology of object permanence, it explores time as phosphene-like: continuously arriving and vanishing, as ethereal matter does. Using Javascript to generate randomised labyrinths of language, I approach time as psychological substance for dreaming, a non-linear narrative continuum teeming with potentiality. Here, language acts as imprint, ghost stamps on the landscapes of time, evoking the impermanence to permanence.
