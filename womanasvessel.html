Woman as Vessel, 2024
Digital archive

Woman as vessel researches artifacts at the MET depicting feminised bodies that are historically framed as a vessels for reproduction and caregiving. Through this collaborative archive, we explore how the vessel might be reclaimed as a technology of agency, reimagined by those it was meant to contain. The work links to my interdisciplinary practice of creating poetic, digital, and visual sites that transform inherited structures into spaces of devotion, speculation, and liberation.
