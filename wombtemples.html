Womb Temples, 2024
Web Sculpture

Womb Temples reimagines the womb and the temple–two historically disrupted vessels–as sites of alternate spiritual realities and mythical gender hybridities beyond the inventions of social borders like gender, caste, religion. Through reappropriating cultural records via technological glitch and browser performance, womb temples is a ceremony of intimacy with our collective pasts. It engages with alternative myth-making as means to queer fugitivity,speculating ways remixed archives can challenge alterations to history.
